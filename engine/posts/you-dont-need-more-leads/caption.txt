The instinct when jobs are thin is to buy more leads. More ad spend, another directory, a new lead service.

It is the most expensive way to solve the wrong problem.

If a lead rings and nobody answers, buying two more of them just means two more that ring out. The bucket has holes in it and you are turning the hose up.

Work out where yours actually leak before you spend another dollar filling it. There are usually only four places to look.

Calls that ring out while you are on a job. Enquiries that come in after hours. Quotes that go out and never get chased. Old customers nobody has spoken to in a year.

Every one of those is a lead you already paid for and did not convert. Fixing them costs less than replacing them, and the fix keeps working after you stop paying for it.
