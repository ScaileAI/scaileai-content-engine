Every name in your old job records cost you something. An ad, a lead fee, a referral you looked after, a van wrap, a week of answering the phone.

You paid that once. Most contractors never spend it again.

The furnace you serviced three winters ago is three winters older. The customer whose water heater you replaced has neighbours. The estimate somebody sat on last spring is still sitting there.

None of that needs new advertising. It needs someone to go back through the list and start a conversation, which is exactly the kind of work that never happens because there is always a job on today.

That is the cheapest revenue in your business and the easiest to ignore, because nothing bad happens when you skip it. It just quietly does not arrive.

Save this, then go and count how many names are actually in there. The number is usually bigger than people expect.
