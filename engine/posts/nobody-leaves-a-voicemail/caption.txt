Did you know almost nobody leaves a voicemail any more?

Think about your own behaviour. You ring a business, it goes to the recorded message, and you hang up and ring the next one. You do not sit through the beep and explain your problem to a machine.

Your customers are the same. So the voicemail box that feels like a safety net is not catching anything. It is just where the call goes to be quietly lost.

And because there is no message, there is no record. You do not get a list on Monday of the people who gave up. Those calls do not appear anywhere. They are invisible, and invisible problems never get fixed.

That is what makes this one worth checking. Not the messages you have to return. The calls that ended in silence.

Save this one and go look at your missed call log.
