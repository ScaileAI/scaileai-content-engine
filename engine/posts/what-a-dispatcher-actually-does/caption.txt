Picking up the phone is the easy part.

What the caller actually wants is a day and a time. Getting there means asking where they are, what has failed, whether it is an emergency, and whether you cover that area at all.

Then somebody has to write it down, put it on the calendar, and make sure it is still there tomorrow.

That is four or five steps, every call, while you are holding a wrench.

It is not complicated work. It is just work that has to happen at the exact moment you are least able to do it.
