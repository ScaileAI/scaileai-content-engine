Did you know you can simply ask the people who did not hire you why they went elsewhere?

Almost nobody does it. It feels like reopening a loss. But a short, genuinely curious message a week later gets answered more often than you would think, because most people quite like being asked their opinion when nothing is being sold to them.

And the answers are rarely what contractors assume. It is usually not price. It is that somebody else answered first, or turned up when they said they would, or explained the job in a way that made sense.

Those are all fixable. Price mostly is not.

The reason this is worth doing is that you are otherwise guessing. Every contractor has a theory about why they lose work, and the theory is almost always price, and it is almost always wrong.

Save this and ask the next three.
