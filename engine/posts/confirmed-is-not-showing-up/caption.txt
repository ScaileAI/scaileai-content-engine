A booked job is not a job. It is a promise from somebody who has not thought about you since.

They booked nine days ago. In that time the drip slowed down, somebody had a look at it for free, and the urgency that made them call has gone.

So you drive out and nobody answers the door.

That gap costs twice. There is the wasted trip, and there is the job you turned down to hold the slot. The second one is bigger and you never see it.

The fix is not chasing people or taking deposits. It is a reminder the day before and another on the morning, which gives them an easy way to cancel early. An early cancellation is a slot you can resell. Silence on the doorstep is not.

None of this is complicated. It is admin that has to happen on a specific day, which is exactly the kind of thing that never happens when you are on the tools.

Save this one for the next time you get a Tuesday gap.
