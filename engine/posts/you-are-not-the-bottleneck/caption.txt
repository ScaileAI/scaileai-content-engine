A lot of advice aimed at contractors quietly assumes the problem is discipline. Better systems, tighter routines, get up earlier.

Usually it is not that.

You have two hands and one phone, and the moments when the phone matters most are exactly the moments both hands are busy. That is not a character flaw. It is arithmetic.

The useful question is not how to be better at doing two things at once. It is which of those two things actually needs you.

The repair needs you. The person on the phone needs an answer, a price and a time, and they do not much mind where those come from as long as they arrive quickly.

That is the part worth handing over. Not the trade. The front desk.

Follow along if that is the kind of thing you think about.
