A water heater does not check your opening hours.

Somebody rang on Saturday morning while you were on the sideline. It went to voicemail. They did not leave one, because almost nobody leaves one any more. They rang the next name on the list.

You never knew that call happened. There is no record of it, no missed lead in a spreadsheet, nothing to review on Monday. It is invisible, and invisible problems do not get fixed.

This is the part people get wrong about weekend cover. It is not that you should be working weekends. It is that the person calling has water on the floor right now and will keep dialling until somebody picks up.

Answering is not the same as working. Something can pick up, ask what is wrong, and put them in your Monday diary while you stay on the sideline.
