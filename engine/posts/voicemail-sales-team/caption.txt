Your voicemail is not a sales team.

It does not qualify anybody. It does not book anything. It does not call them back. It sits there and holds a message you will listen to after the customer has already hired someone else.

And that is the best case. Most people who ring a contractor and get a recording simply hang up and try the next name on the list. No message, no record, no trace that the call ever happened.

So you go looking for the problem in your ads, or your pricing, or your reviews. The problem was a phone nobody could reach for forty minutes because you were under a house.

Answering every call yourself is not the fix either. You cannot be on a job and on the phone at the same time, and hiring someone to sit by it rarely pays for itself at your volume.

The fix is something that answers on the first ring, asks what you would ask, and puts the job on your calendar while you keep working.
