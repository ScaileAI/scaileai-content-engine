Most contractors I talk to built the business on referrals, and they are right to be proud of it. It means the work is good.

The trouble is what happens in a slow month.

Referrals arrive when they arrive. You cannot ask them to arrive faster, you cannot turn them up in February, and the month they go quiet is usually the month you needed them most. That is not a strategy, that is weather.

And it hides things. When the phone rings on its own you never find out how many people looked you up, could not reach you, and moved on. The referrals cover the leak.

None of this means chasing strangers. The most reliable work is still the people who already know you, contacted on purpose rather than by chance.
