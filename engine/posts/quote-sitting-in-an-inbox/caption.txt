You did the site visit. You worked out the price. You sent the quote.

Then what.

For most contractors the honest answer is nothing. The quote goes out and every move after that belongs to the customer, who is busy, who has three other things happening, and who never actually decided against you.

That is the part worth sitting with. Most quotes do not lose to a competitor. They lose to silence.

One follow up three days later, asking whether anything has changed rather than whether they have decided, restarts more jobs than any discount will.

The reason it does not happen is not laziness. It is that you were under a sink when the reminder would have been useful, and by the evening it was gone.
