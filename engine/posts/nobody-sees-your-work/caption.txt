Did you know the best argument for hiring you is the work you already did and never photographed?

Every contractor has a hundred jobs behind them that looked genuinely good when they finished. Almost none of them exist anywhere. No photo, no record, nothing a stranger could ever look at.

So when somebody is deciding between you and two other names, they have nothing to go on except a review from three years ago and a gut feeling.

It takes about fifteen seconds. Before you pack up, one photo of the finished work. Not staged, not clever. Just what it looks like now compared to what they were living with.

That is a portfolio built out of jobs you were already doing.

The reason it does not happen is not laziness. It is that the moment to take it is the moment you are carrying tools to the van.

Follow along if this is the kind of thing you keep meaning to sort out.
