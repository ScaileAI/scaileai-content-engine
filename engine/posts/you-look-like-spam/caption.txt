Did you know your own phone number might be showing up to customers as a suspected spam call?

Carriers score numbers automatically. A number that makes a lot of short outbound calls to people who do not pick up starts to look like a telemarketer, and that is exactly what a contractor returning missed calls looks like from the outside.

Once that happens, the label appears on the customer screen before they decide. They do not answer. You assume they went elsewhere. You are ringing them and they are watching it ring.

This is worth ten minutes. Ring your own number from a phone that has never called you. Ask a customer what comes up when you call. Check whether the number you actually dial out from is the one on your van and your website, because often it is not.

It is a quiet one, and it does not announce itself.
